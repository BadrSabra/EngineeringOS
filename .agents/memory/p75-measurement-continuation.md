---
name: P7.5 measurement continuation
description: Identity and authority boundaries for recovering runtime-start measurements after execution-attempt rotation.
---

A cross-attempt P7.5 continuation must keep the immutable source registration separate from each read-only measurement identity. Bind them to the same durable execution, Mission, Goal, and plan/project/environment revisions; require later attempts and distinct Episodes; restrict observations to server-owned `runtime.status`; never replay `runtime.start`. Registration, observation, stored-result, and current-recovery Episodes can have different owners. Carry each exact owner identity (including the prior observation's continuation ID) and close every historical owner plus the current Episode atomically.

Continuation receipts are not calibration-v1 results. Keep old unresolved registrations unresolved; only a separately reviewed policy/scope version and evaluator may admit continuation outcomes into a future cohort. A typed receipt alone does not authorize a runtime read or prove a retained observation.

**Why:** Resume rotates attempt, Episode, and continuation identities. The recovered observation may belong to an older request even when the current worker creates a new request; conflating those identities can mis-bind evidence, while leaving any owner Episode open makes durable recovery appear unfinished. An in-memory runtime fixture proves observer semantics but not the supervisor service boundary or survival of its child after API-worker loss. Silently counting continuation results would change the calibration protocol after outcomes are visible.

**How to apply:** Append new request/result events under the current attempt's lease and Episode; validate the source registration, observation-owning request and row, and any stored result/request pair against their exact identities and scope. Terminalize all distinct historical owner Episodes and the current Episode using attempt-local event sequences in one transaction under the current live lease. Fail closed on cancellation, revision drift, duplicate/conflicting results, or missing proof. For cross-process runtime recovery proof, use the DB-backed production runtime manager through the managed supervisor listener; an in-memory manager alone does not establish that integration. Keep calibration eligibility disabled until a versioned evaluator explicitly accepts the new protocol.
