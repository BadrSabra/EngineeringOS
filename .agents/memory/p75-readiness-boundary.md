---
name: P7.5 collection readiness boundary
description: Authority limits for the read-only pre-collection readiness report.
---

Keep P7.5 readiness reports and evidence packs diagnostic only. A clean machine report must not start a cohort or authorize collection. Mission IDs and the fixed held-out partition are metadata, not proof of independent sampling or an unused holdout. A deterministic pack may bind protocol/version hashes and preflight evidence, but it must keep operator and reviewer claims missing until a trusted authority source exists. Episode IDs in a registration payload do not prove ledger ownership: verify the persisted event row, payload hash, actor/correlation, parent Episode identity, and contiguous stream before marking machine provenance verified. That verification proves agreement with the current database snapshot, not independent immutability or human approval. Do not accept arbitrary caller booleans or unverified strings as reviews.

**Why:** P7.5 records prove technical scope and result integrity but do not by themselves prove durable Episode event ownership, operator-controlled reset, independence, or whether outcome data were previously used for tuning. A database-backed provenance check closes only the machine-record binding gap; it cannot create a trusted human authority or turn hashes into an immutable signature.

**How to apply:** Read P7.5 records from the durable Episode ledger in one repeatable-read, read-only transaction; recompute the readiness report from those rows, and bind event IDs/hashes and Episode stream metadata into the internal pack. Missing, conflicting, or over-bound snapshots block provenance; an empty scope remains review-required. Any future endpoint or UI may surface blockers but must remain read-only until a separate reviewable authority contract exists. Keep `fixed_safe_probe` and calibration v1 unchanged; continuation receipts remain excluded. The pack must remain `BLOCKED` or `REVIEW_REQUIRED` with `collectionAuthorized=false`.
