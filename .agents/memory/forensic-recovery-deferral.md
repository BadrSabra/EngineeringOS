---
name: Forensic recovery deferral
description: Structured forensic responses with retained evidence must reach the recovery contract before objective incompleteness is terminalized.
---

When a structured forensic provider response contains retained source evidence, an objective claim gap is not sufficient reason to terminate the tool loop before chat-level contract recovery. Defer that terminal classification so bounded recovery can issue targeted reads and preserve the correct scope-failure evidence.

**Why:** Early `incomplete` termination bypasses forensic recovery, suppresses targeted-read telemetry, and can turn a recoverable contract failure into an untraceable objective block.

**How to apply:** Keep evidence-free or text-free incomplete runs terminal, but allow non-empty structured forensic candidates to continue through contract validation, targeted recovery, objective gating, and telemetry attachment.
