# Stop validation runs from hanging before code delivery

## What & Why
Two isolated repair-validation scenarios currently reach the 120-second test timeout, leaving operators unsure whether a candidate is still running or blocked. This prevents a trustworthy apply/promotion path.

## Done looks like
- Every server-owned validation attempt has finite process and overall deadlines.
- Timeout outcomes persist as explicit blocked/unavailable receipts with safe diagnostics.
- The isolated validation scenarios complete without harness timeouts.
- Candidate, revision, and evidence binding remain enforced.
