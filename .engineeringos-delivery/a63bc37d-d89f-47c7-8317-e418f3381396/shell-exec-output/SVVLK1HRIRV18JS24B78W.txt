## What & Why
The blocking release check now proves structured-review fallback and incomplete handling deterministically without credentials. A separate opt-in campaign would validate that provider-specific outage and model-availability responses map to the same safe operator outcome when exercised against a disposable environment.

## Done looks like
- The campaign is explicitly opt-in and never runs during normal provider-free release validation
- Reasoning-only, agent-harness, rate-limit, empty, and malformed provider outcomes are captured without persisting provider secrets or raw provider messages
- The resulting receipt distinguishes fallback success with a cited finding from terminal incomplete status
